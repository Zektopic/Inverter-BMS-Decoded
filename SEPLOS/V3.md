# Seplos V3 BMS (Modbus RTU)

> Status: 🌐 Community. Built from two community register maps that partly disagree; not yet checked against captures.

| | |
|---|---|
| Port | RS485, Modbus RTU, 19200 8N1 |
| Address | DIP switch; all switches off answers on address **0** |
| Does not answer | the V2 ASCII protocol (`~20...` frames are ignored) |

## Registers

| Block | FC | Start | Count | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PIA | 0x04 | 0x1000 | 8 | voltage 10 mV, current 10 mA (signed, + charge), remaining 10 mAh, full 10 mAh, total discharged 10 Ah, SOC 0.1 %, SOH 0.1 %, cycles |
| PIB | 0x04 | 0x1100 | 26 | cells 1-16 (mV) at 0x1100-0x110F; cell temps 1-4 (0.1 K) at 0x1110-0x1113; two more temps at 0x1118/0x1119 |
| PIC | 0x01 | 0x1200 | 144 coils | 0x1240 discharging, 0x1241 charging, 0x1244 idle, 0x1278 discharge FET, 0x1279 charge FET; other coils: see below |

## Where sources disagree

- 0x1118 / 0x1119: [syssi/esphome-seplos-bms](https://github.com/syssi/esphome-seplos-bms) labels them ambient / MOSFET; the
  [seplos-v3-ha-modbus](https://github.com/rolandfiege69-svg/seplos-v3-ha-modbus) map labels them MOSFET / ambient. Treat both as unlabelled until confirmed.
- Coils: esphome decodes alarm bytes at the start of the coil block; the HA map (verified on one pack) places state and FET bits
  at 0x1240/0x1278. Only the HA bits are confirmed. Captures from a real pack are welcome (see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
